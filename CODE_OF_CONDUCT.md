# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and respectful environment for everyone who
participates in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption
are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports
will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
